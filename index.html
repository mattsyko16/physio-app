Traffic light rehab guide

GREEN ✅

  • Mild discomfort only
  • No worsening later
  • Normal next morning
  • Action: continue full plan

AMBER ⚠️

  • Ache later same day
  • Slight stiffness next morning
  • Action: reduce load 30–50%
  • Drop external rotation first
  • Lighter grip, slow tempo

RED ⛔

  • Sharp/increasing pain
  • Worse next morning
  • Action: stop resisted work
  • Skip elbow loading
  • Skip resisted ankle work
  • Keep gentle mobility only

Flare rule: reduce for 3–5 days, keep mobility, then resume once settled.
